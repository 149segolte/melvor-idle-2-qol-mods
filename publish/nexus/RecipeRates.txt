DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Recipe Rates[/b][/size]
Which recipe gives more XP per hour: the one that takes 60 seconds for 10 XP, or the one that takes 20 seconds for 3? A recipe shows the XP of one action and, somewhere else, how long the action takes. This does the division for you, for every recipe of the list at once.

[size=4][b]In the recipe list[/b][/size]
Open Smithing, Crafting, Fletching, Runecrafting, Beastcrafting, Cooking, Firemaking or Herblore. Under the XP tag of every recipe is its [b]XP per hour[/b]. The one in gold is the best of the list. Nothing has to be started: the numbers are there for recipes you have never made.

[size=4][b]In the XP tooltip[/b][/size]
Put the pointer on a recipe's XP tag. Under the game's own lines is "Per hour (estimate)":
[list]
[*][b]Skill XP[/b] per hour.
[*]Every [b]product[/b] you make in an hour, doubling included.
[*]Every [b]ingredient[/b] an hour uses, preservation included.
[*][b]Sell value[/b] of an hour's products.
[*]How long one takes, and the success chance when it is not certain.
[*][b]Materials for ...[/b]: how long what is in your bank lasts at that pace.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for the recipe, which already contain every bonus you have (equipment, potions, mastery). Chances count as their long-run average: the chance to skip a step, to double a product, to preserve an ingredient, to fail a cook. Herblore's preparing, heating and stirring are spread over the bottles of a batch.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many actions, not over ten.
[*]Cooking assumes a failed attempt gives neither XP nor food.
[*]Sell value is not profit: the ingredients are not taken off.
[*]Only skill XP is shown, not mastery XP.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\RecipeRates\RecipeRates.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\RecipeRates[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game. It gives no items, XP or bonuses.
